everything bunker_ does, in plain words. no roadmap fluff.

01 what is bunker_

a panic button for solana. you set one rule. when sol crashes, your sol is swapped to usdc. when it bounces, it's swapped back. you don't have to watch the chart.

02 the rule

two numbers, that's all:

hide   = how far sol may fall from its top before you leave   (e.g. 12%)
return = how big a bounce from the bottom brings you back     (e.g. 8%)

sol in wallet ──(sol −12% from top)──▶ usdc
usdc          ──(sol +8% from bottom)─▶ sol

the top keeps moving up while you hold, so the exit follows the price up. the bottom keeps moving down while you're out, so you only come back after a real bounce.

03 your keys

  • orders are placed from your own wallet. bunker_ never holds your funds.
  • every order is visible on-chain. you can cancel it any time.
  • no deposits, no vault, nothing to withdraw.

04 why usdc

the bunker is a regulated dollar stablecoin, not a coin we print. a protection tool that invents its own "stable" money is just another risk.

05 the token

bunker_ is a meme token on solana. holders use the tool free; everyone else pays a small fee per trigger.

06 what works today

  • live: the app replays the rule on real sol prices, updated every second.

07 risks

  • gaps: in a fast crash the price can jump past your trigger, so you exit lower than planned.
  • chop: in a sideways market the rule can sell low and buy back higher, again and again.
  • usdc: a stablecoin carries issuer risk.
  • the token: meme tokens are volatile. only use what you can afford to lose.

nothing here is financial advice. past prices don't predict the future.

08 faq

do i need to keep the site open?

no. once orders are on-chain they fire on their own.

can bunker_ take my sol?

no. the orders come from your wallet and only swap between sol and usdc in that wallet.

what numbers should i pick?

try them in the app on past prices. tighter rules exit sooner but trade more often.

which wallets?

phantom, solflare, backpack, any standard solana wallet.
